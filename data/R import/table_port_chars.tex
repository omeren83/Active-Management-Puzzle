EW: equal-weighted; VW: lagged-TNA-weighted. Returns in monthly \%. EW Sharpe is the annualised Sharpe ratio of the quintile portfolio's equal-weighted \textit{gross} excess return, $\sqrt{12}\cdot\overline{r^{\text{EW,g}}_{q,t}-r^{f}_{t}}/\sigma(r^{\text{EW,g}}_{q,t}-r^{f}_{t})$. Sample: Incubation-corrected panel (Evans 2010), no date cap; performance-comparison subsample per flagged\_funds.xlsx.
