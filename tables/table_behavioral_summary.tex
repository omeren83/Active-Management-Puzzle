For H2 the predicted sign is negative (disposition), and the opposite sign is the margin-call alternative of Brunnermeier and Pedersen (2009). The ActR2 row of H3 is a rational-null control: activeness is not a lottery feature. Each hypothesis is estimated on its own aligned sample (all regressors of its specifications non-missing), so $N$ differs across hypotheses; the H2 put--call rows use the shorter put--call ratio window. Primary specifications use contemporaneous state variables, fund FE for H1--H3 and Lipper $\times$ year-month FE for H4, with two-way clustering on Ticker and calendar month (Petersen 2009).
